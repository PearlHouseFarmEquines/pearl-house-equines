Why Swim Your Horse?

Swimming is one of the most effective low-impact conditioning tools in the equine world.

Fitness Without Concussion

Water supports your horse's weight, delivering intense cardiovascular and muscular work with almost no strain on joints, tendons, and hooves.

Balanced Muscle Development

Water resistance works the topline, hindquarters, and core symmetrically — engaging muscles differently than under-saddle work and helping correct imbalances.

Rehabilitation & Recovery

Trusted by rehab facilities and racehorse trainers alike, swimming rebuilds fitness while protecting healing legs from load — and doubles as cooling recovery in summer.

Mental Refreshment

The lake breaks up ring routine. Many horses genuinely love the water, and swimming can restore enthusiasm in horses gone sour or stale.

A Gift for Senior Horses

For older horses managing arthritis and inflammation, swimming is one of the kindest ways to stay fit. The water's buoyancy takes pressure off aching joints while cool lake water helps soothe inflammation — letting senior horses build and keep the muscle that supports their joints, without the pounding of ring work. Many owners find their older partners move more freely and comfortably with regular swim sessions.

Lake Champlain is our backyard

Few places in the country offer trail-in lake access for horses. Here, the lake offers a unique experience — a place where horses relax, bodies heal, and people discover what it feels like to move through the water beside a thousand-pound partner who can develop trust.
